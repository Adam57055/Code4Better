About Code4Good's founder:

Adam Twam is a student developer based in Greenville, South Carolina. He has been involved in several volunteer tech organizations and has had a

desire to use tech for civic involvement.

Over the past 6 months, he has consistently maintained a GitHub portfolio of over 90 repositories, including personal projects and open-source contributions.

After noticing some non-profits being disadvantaged through no digital traffic, he wanted to perform the service himself using his newly formed skill.
