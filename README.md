# nonprofit.help

# What We Do
This software aims to help non-profits succeed when it comes to needing a technological aspect in the form of websites. Websites are an excellent way to gain customer traffic as digital presence becomes increasingly more common in today's world.

# Mission
Our personal values are to use tech skills to build what non-profits benefit from the most: digital traffic. Through our creation of personalized websites, non-profits can expect more traction for no price.

# Background
As digital presence is increasing drastically among businesses in this era, non-profits that do not do the same are at a huge disadvantage. They will not receive digital traction which could establish a more prominent presence and make a better image. Noticing this, the founder believed that there should be an organization that combats this issue and offers personalized templates made for such non-profits.
